SEKVOYA

Sekvoya is a Novi Sad–based psychedelic/world ensemble blending Eastern melodic traditions with Western groove and arrangement sensibilities. With vintage tones, hypnotic rhythms, and a distinctly Balkan emotional color, Sekvoya creates immersive soundscapes that feel both ancient and forward-looking. Their music sits at the crossroads of Anatolian rock, psychedelic rock, and contemporary world/ethno music.

BIOGRAPHY

Hailing from Novi Sad, Serbia, Sekvoya music transports listeners into a rich sonic journey where traditional Balkan atmospheres meet contemporary psychedelic grooves. Through this blend, Sekvoya offers a renewed perspective on regional melodic heritage, reframing it within expansive, swirling, dream-like soundscapes.

The project was born in Soundbubble Studio, where Ivan laid the foundation as the sole creator and producer. His early experiments focused on capturing vintage timbres, psychedelic colors, and swampy, atmospheric textures that transcend typical genre boundaries. As the project grew, Ivan invited like-minded musicians to join the journey, forming a five-piece ensemble that added depth, energy, and new creative layers to the sound. Today, Sekvoya stands as a collective dedicated to exploring cultural roots through a modern, explorative sonic identity.

SOUND & INFLUENCES

Sekvoya’s rhythm section draws influence from the desert-blues trance of Tinariwen and Mdou Moctar, blended with the laid-back funk of Khruangbin, the raw groove of Karl Hector, and the cinematic global textures of Sven Wunder. Traditional Balkan rhythmic motifs are woven into this hybrid, forming a middle place between Eastern and Western pulse.

Melodically, the band draws from Serbian rock icons like Vlatko Stefanovski and Radomir Mihajlović Točak, while also embracing contemporary global psych acts such as Derya Yildirim, Altın Gün, Satellites, and Bab L’Bluz. The result is a vibrant, soulful, and highly textured melodic language that feels both familiar and mystical.
